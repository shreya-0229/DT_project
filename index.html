ThinkFirst is an AI Learning Companion built by students, for students. It gives you hints before answers, makes you try first, and shows you honestly how dependent on AI you've become.

The problem we solve

Fast answers, shallow learning

Students use generative AI for assignments, coding, summaries and exam prep because it's fast. But the habit quietly backfires:

  • Ready-made answers get copied without understanding them
  • Students rarely try on their own first before asking AI
  • Nobody can see how dependent they've become — until exam day

The result: weaker independent problem-solving and fragile conceptual understanding.

Our solution

An AI that makes you think first

ThinkFirst flips the order — your brain goes first, AI goes second:

  • Hints before answers — three levels of nudges, not solutions
  • A full explanation unlocks only after your own attempt
  • Concepts explained step-by-step, with analogies and examples
  • An AI Dependency Score tracks AI use vs. independent work
  • Personal feedback and a quick understanding check close the loop
